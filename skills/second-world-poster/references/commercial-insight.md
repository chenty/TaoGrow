# Commercial Insight

## Feature to value
1. Feature: what is offered?
2. Functional benefit: what becomes easier, faster, safer, clearer, or more reliable?
3. Emotional benefit: what worry is reduced or confidence created?
4. Business value: what cost, risk, time, coordination effort, or opportunity is affected?
5. Evidence: what supports the claim?

Never invent evidence or turn an aspiration into a guaranteed result.

## Audience lenses
- Business owner: cost, risk, continuity, efficiency, reputation, retention, long-term value.
- HR/procurement: coordination workload, process clarity, vendor reliability, employee experience.
- Employee/end user: convenience, dignity, clear information, comfort, trust.
- Individual consumer: problem-solving, confidence, ease, service experience, transparent value.
- Brand audience: positioning, differentiation, credibility, emotional connection.

## Angle selection
Choose distinct angles that fit the audience and selling point:
- Need or pain point
- Desired outcome / customer benefit
- Trust / proof / expertise
- Service experience
- Efficiency / convenience
- Care / reassurance
- Long-term value / future vision
- Differentiation
- Call to action

Example: “员工健康，企业未来”
- Employee wellbeing as a foundation for sustainable business.
- Organized checkups that reduce HR coordination burden.
- A respectful, reassuring examination experience.
- Clear follow-up information that helps employees understand their health.
- Long-term health attention as part of responsible workplace culture.

Only describe services actually provided. Do not imply diagnosis, ongoing health management, special turnaround times, or guaranteed outcomes without evidence.
