# Copywriting and Claim Integrity

## Headlines
Express one clear customer-facing value. Prefer concrete wording over hype. Examples:
- “让员工少一点等待”
- “把健康关怀落到每一次服务”
- “让团检安排更有序”
- “看见需求，服务才有价值”

Avoid unsupported claims such as “行业第一”, “全城最快”, “百分百通过”, “零风险”, or “保证治愈”, as well as fabricated statistics, awards, credentials, testimonials, and customer counts.

## Regulated categories
For healthcare, finance, legal, education, and safety:
- Avoid guarantees and absolute promises.
- Do not imply diagnosis or treatment from a screening service.
- Verify qualifications, scope, prices, turnaround times, and eligibility with the user.
- If evidence is absent, use modest, factual language.

## Text rendering
Image models may render Chinese inaccurately. If exact typography matters:
1. Generate the visual with a clean text-safe area.
2. Supply exact headline and supporting copy separately.
3. Add typography in a design/editor tool.
Do not claim the text is accurate unless inspected.
