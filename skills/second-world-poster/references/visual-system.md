# Second World Visual System

## Composition
- Vertical 3:4 canvas by default.
- Upper approximately 50%: faithful real photograph.
- Lower approximately 50%: warm ivory paper-like world.
- A source-specific transition connects both zones.

## Source continuity
The imaginative extension must emerge from something visible in the photo: contour, silhouette, shadow, reflection, path, floor line, architecture, plant, fabric, hand, tool, machine detail, or light.

## Graphic language
- Restrained black hand-drawn linework.
- Limited paper-cut elements or small fragments.
- Subtle paper grain.
- Generous negative space.
- Clear typography hierarchy.
- Consistent palette and visual language across the series.

## Consistency and variation
Keep palette, paper texture, typography, and graphic restraint consistent. Vary the source element, metaphor, lower-world composition, focal point, and spatial rhythm.

## Image integrity
Do not materially alter the upper photo. Do not merge source photos. Do not create fake signage, logos, badges, or institutional marks.
