# Single Poster Generation Prompt

Create ONE independent vertical 3:4 marketing poster in the Second World visual storytelling style.

Campaign:
- Selling point: [SELLING_POINT]
- Target audience: [AUDIENCE]
- This poster's unique angle: [ANGLE]
- Main message: [MESSAGE]
- Headline: [HEADLINE]
- Supporting copy: [SUPPORTING_COPY]

Source photo:
Use the supplied photo as the real photographic upper section. Preserve the original subject, environment, lighting, objects, and factual details. Only proportional crop/scale is allowed. Do not repaint, replace, beautify, or redesign the upper photo.

Composition:
- Upper approximately 50%: faithful source photograph; no invented objects, signage, logos, or credentials.
- Lower approximately 50%: warm ivory paper-like background.
- Extend [SOURCE_ELEMENT] from the real photograph across the boundary into [SECOND_WORLD_METAPHOR].
- Transition edge must be shaped by the source photo, not a generic torn-paper strip.
- Restrained black hand-drawn linework and a few paper-cut details.
- Generous negative space and clear hierarchy.

Series consistency:
Match the shared palette, paper texture, typography hierarchy, and restraint of the other posters, while keeping this poster's metaphor and composition distinct.

Constraints:
One standalone poster only. No collage, grid, contact sheet, merged photos, unrelated fantasy imagery, generic portals, altered upper photo, fabricated facts, statistics, testimonials, guarantees, logos, or certifications. Keep text concise and legible. If Chinese text rendering is uncertain, leave clean text-safe space for post-production.
